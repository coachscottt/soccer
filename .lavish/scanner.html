Fair Value Scanner

Market-implied fair values: a Dixon–Coles score matrix calibrated per match to the devigged sharp 1X2 and totals line. Team xG = the calibrated scoring rates (home λ – away μ). Model H/D/A = your independent v2 engine (xG-trained, no market inputs) — logged every scan and graded against close and results. Completed games drop off automatically and stay tracked in the CLV ledger.
